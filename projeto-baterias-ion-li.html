Li-Ion Battery Storage

Lithium-ion battery energy storage system (BESS) integrated with photovoltaic generation for Black Start and emergency power supply to critical auxiliary services in transmission substations.

Full Project Overview

Scope and Technical Details

ANEEL Project Code: PD-07351-0001/2022 · Duration: 36 months (2023–2026) · Pilot Substation: SE Trindade (Goiás, Brazil).

Objectives

Deploy a hybrid emergency and routine energy supply system for auxiliary services in transmission substations, combining a 200 kWh lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery energy storage system with a 50 kWp solar photovoltaic plant to ensure uninterrupted substation operation and Black Start capability.

Innovation and Originality

Direct coupling of the Li-ion BESS and solar generation via a non-isolated buck DC-DC converter operating under voltage control. During grid contingencies, the system operates seamlessly in islanded (off-grid) mode, energizing critical DC and AC auxiliary loads while recharging the battery bank to extend substation autonomy without relying on fossil diesel generators.

Critical Factors and Benefits

Enhancing grid reliability in the National Interconnected System (SIN), mitigating power interruption risks, avoiding regulatory Variable Penalty (PV) costs for transmission concessionaires, and decarbonizing auxiliary substation power by eliminating diesel emissions.

Pilot Deployment Site

Installed at the SE Trindade Substation (Goiás, Brazil), connecting switchgear, converter cubicles, and the battery container directly to the auxiliary service bus of Firminópolis Transmissão's control house, interfacing 380/220 VAC and 125 VDC systems.

Scientific and Academic Output

Publication in peer-reviewed international scientific journals, including a comprehensive Systematic Literature Review published in Energies (MDPI, 2025), alongside human capital development supporting one MSc dissertation and one PhD thesis in power systems and energy storage.
