Reference Price Database and Asset Useful Life

Updating the Reference Price Database methodology and tools to enable faster, more accurate updates and improve investment estimates.

Project Details

Scope and Details

Deliverables

technical reports on reference prices and asset useful life; methodology reparameterization; update tools; regulatory useful-life and replacement models; training.

Stages and Milestones

regulatory assessment; historical fit and trend analysis; metrics and governance; reparameterization; update tools; feedback, workshops, and go-live; audit and final report.

Critical Factors

tracking ABRATE and A&M activities; providing data and analyzing consolidated information.
