Book · Pecém H₂V Project

A Path to the Low-Carbon Hydrogen Market in Brazil

Editors: Nivalde de Castro, Ana Carolina Chaves, Mauricio Moszkowicz, Eduardo Serra, and Sayonara Eliziário. GESEL/UFRJ, in partnership with EDP, as part of the Pecém H₂V project.

This book provides an overview of Brazil’s low-carbon hydrogen market, its opportunities, and its challenges across industries such as manufacturing, transportation, and energy.

Markets and Industrial Development

Green Industrial Development in Brazil: Opportunities in the National and International Markets for Renewable Hydrogen and Derivatives

Luiza Masseno de Pinho Santiago Leal, Ana Carolina Chaves Catolico, Kalyne Silva Brito, and Vinicius José Novaes Braz da Costa.

This paper examines the conditions for developing a renewable hydrogen and derivatives industry in Brazil. It discusses market opportunities, local production, and the potential to connect the energy, industrial, and export sectors.

Transportation and Storage

A Comparative Analysis of the Technical Parameters of Ammonia and Methanol as Hydrogen Carriers

Sofia Paoli, Kalyne Brito, José Vinícius Freitas, and Thereza Aquino.

This paper compares ammonia, methanol, and liquid hydrogen as transportation and storage options. Ammonia and methanol can store more energy per volume, but each requires specific safety measures and infrastructure and involves conversion losses.

Read the full article (PDF)

Financing and Public Policy · 2022

The Role of Financing in Green Hydrogen Projects: The German, Australian and Brazilian Cases

Thereza Aquino, Roberto Ivo, Vinicius Botelho, Ana Carolina Chaves, Lourival Teixeira, Cayo Moraes, Diego Rebouças, and Willamy Conde. 5th International Conference on Energy and Environment, 2022.

This study compares financing structures for green hydrogen projects in Germany, Australia, and Brazil. It highlights the role of public support in reducing risk and enabling investment.

Read the full article (PDF)

Industrial Hubs and the Hydrogen Economy · 2024

The Role of Industrial Clusters in the Development of the Hydrogen Economy: A Comparison between United States, Europe, Australia

Kalyne Brito, Ana Carolina Chaves, Thereza Aquino, Luiza Masseno, and Caroline Chantre. 6th International Conference on Energy and Environment, Guimarães, Portugal, 2024.

This paper examines hydrogen hub policies and experience in the United States, Europe, and Australia. It discusses how infrastructure, proximity between producers and consumers, and governance can accelerate the transition.
